Abstract

Remote sensing visual grounding (RSVG) aims to localize the object referred to by a natural-language expression in aerial or satellite imagery. Recent multimodal large language models (MLLMs) make unified box-level grounding feasible, but general-purpose models do not reliably produce oriented bounding boxes (OBBs) and often fall back to horizontal bounding box (HBB) outputs. Jointly training a single model for HBB and OBB prediction is also challenging, because the two tasks require different levels of semantic grounding and geometric precision.

We propose GeoBox-R1, a two-stage training paradigm for unified box-level RSVG. It first performs curriculum-guided supervised fine-tuning, ordering training data from HBB grounding to OBB grounding and finally to HBB-to-OBB Chain-of-Thought supervision, establishing unified box-level grounding progressively. It then applies geometric reinforcement learning via Group Reward-Decoupled Normalization Policy Optimization (GDPO), using two rule-based geometric rewards — Rotated Intersection over Union (RIoU) and adaptive Wasserstein distance — to refine OBB prediction.

Across 7 HBB and 3 OBB benchmarks, GeoBox-R1 achieves the best macro averages on all three metrics (Acc@0.5, Acc@0.7, and mean overlap) for both tasks. With only 4B parameters, it surpasses state-of-the-art 7B–8B models, leading by 3.68/4.89/2.61 points on HBB and 5.36/7.74/2.89 points on OBB, with the largest gains at the stricter Acc@0.7 threshold.

Method

Stage 1 — Curriculum-guided SFT

Training data is presented easy-to-hard rather than shuffled:

  1. HBB grounding establishes coarse semantic localization.
  2. OBB grounding introduces orientation and aspect-ratio sensitivity.
  3. HBB-to-OBB Chain-of-Thought supervises the intermediate reasoning that refines a coarse horizontal box into a tight oriented box.

Stage 2 — Geometric RL with GDPO

GDPO refines OBB prediction with two rule-based geometric rewards and no learned reward model:

  • Rotated IoU (RIoU) gives direct overlap supervision for oriented boxes.
  • Adaptive Wasserstein distance stays informative when RIoU saturates near zero, which is common for small or elongated targets.

The two rewards are combined with λRIoU=λWD=0.5\lambda_{\mathrm{RIoU}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{WD}} = 0.5λRIoU​=λWD​=0.5.

OBB-only RL does not trade away HBB performance

Although GDPO trains on OBB samples only, HBB macro averages rise from 57.17/40.33/48.82 to 58.78/42.22/50.39. Acc@0.5 improves on six of seven HBB benchmarks, and Acc@0.7 and mIoU improve on all seven. This cross-format transfer is consistent with localization knowledge shared between the two box formats.
